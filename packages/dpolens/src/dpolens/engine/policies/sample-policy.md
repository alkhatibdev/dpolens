# Sample data protection policy

This is a made-up policy for a made-up company, Acme, so that a new DPOLens instance has a
policy of its own to search. Archive it once your organisation's policies are in.

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This policy sets out how Acme collects, uses, keeps and protects personal data, so that
customers and staff can trust what we do with information about them.

1.2 It applies the data protection laws Acme is subject to. Where a law asks for more than
this policy does, the law wins.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This policy covers all personal data Acme handles: about customers, staff, contractors and
anyone who visits our websites.

2.2 It applies to everyone who works for Acme, including contractors and suppliers who handle
personal data on our behalf.

## 3. Principles

Acme only processes personal data:

- lawfully, fairly and in a way people would expect;
- for a purpose we have stated, and not for another one later;
- when we need it, and no more of it than we need;
- for as long as the retention schedule allows, and no longer.

## 4. Retention

4.1 Personal data is deleted or anonymised when its retention period ends.

| Record | Kept for | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer accounts | 2 years after the account closes | Disputes and refunds |
| Payment records | 7 years | Tax law |
| Staff records | 6 years after employment ends | Employment law |
| Support tickets | 1 year after the ticket closes | Quality and complaints |
| Application logs | 90 days | Investigating security incidents |

## 5. Individuals' rights

5.1 Anyone can ask Acme for a copy of the personal data we hold about them. We answer within
one month.

5.2 When someone asks us to delete their personal data, we delete it from live systems within
30 days, and from backups when those backups expire, unless a law requires us to keep it.

5.3 Requests are sent to the Data Protection Officer, who keeps a record of each one and how it
was answered.

## 6. Building and running software

6.1 Logs must not contain passwords, payment card numbers or government identity numbers.
Email addresses and phone numbers in logs are masked.

6.2 Personal data from production is never copied into development or test environments.
Test data is made up or anonymised.

6.3 A new feature that processes personal data in a new way needs a data protection impact
assessment before it is released.

6.4 Analytics use pseudonymous identifiers, never names or email addresses.

## 7. Data breaches

7.1 Anyone who suspects a personal data breach reports it to the Data Protection Officer at
once, even outside working hours.

7.2 The Data Protection Officer assesses each breach and, where the law requires it, notifies
the regulator within 72 hours of Acme becoming aware of it.

## 8. Sharing and transfers

8.1 Personal data is shared with a supplier only under a contract that binds the supplier to
protect it as this policy does.

8.2 Personal data is transferred outside the country where it was collected only where the law
allows it, for example to a country with an adequacy decision or under standard contractual
clauses.

## 9. Review

9.1 The Data Protection Officer reviews this policy every year, and whenever the law or the way
Acme works changes.
