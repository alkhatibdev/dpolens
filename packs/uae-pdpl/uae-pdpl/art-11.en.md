## 1. {#para-1}

The Data Protection Officer shall ensure the extent of compliance of the Controller or the Processor with the application of provisions of this Decree by Law, its Executive Regulations and instructions issued by the Bureau. The Data Protection Officer shall, in particular, undertake the following tasks and powers:

### a. {#pt-a}

Verifying the quality and correctness of the procedures in place at the Controller and the Processor.

### b. {#pt-b}

Receiving requests and complaints related to Personal Data in accordance with provisions of this Decree-Law and its Executive Regulations.

### c. {#pt-c}

Providing technical advice on evaluation procedures and periodic examination of personal data protection systems and intrusion prevention systems at the Controller and Processor, documenting the results of such evaluation and providing appropriate recommendations in this regard, including risk assessment procedures.

### d. {#pt-d}

Acting as a link between the Controller or the Processor, as the case may be, and the Bureau regarding the application of personal data processing provisions stipulated in this Decree by Law.

### e. {#pt-e}

Any other tasks or powers which are determined in accordance with the Executive Regulations of this Decree by Law.

## 2. {#para-2}

The Data Protection Officer shall maintain the confidentiality of information and data it receives in implementation of its duties and powers in accordance with provisions of this Decree by Law and its Executive Regulations and in accordance with the legislations in force in the State.
