## 1. {#para-1}

The Data Subject has the right, by submitting a request to the Controller without any consideration, to obtain the following information:

### a. {#pt-a}

The types of its Personal Data that are being processed.

### b. {#pt-b}

Purposes of processing.

### c. {#pt-c}

Decisions made based on automated processing, including profiling.

### d. {#pt-d}

The targeted sectors or establishments with whom its personal data will be shared from inside and outside the State.

### e. {#pt-e}

Controls and standards for the period of storage and preservation of his/ her personal data.

### f. {#pt-f}

Procedures for correcting, erasing or limiting processing and objection to his/ her personal data.

### g. {#pt-g}

Protection measures for cross-border processing carried out in accordance with Articles (22) and (23) of this By-Law.

### h. {#pt-h}

Actions to be taken in the event of a breach or misuse of his/ her Personal Data, especially if the breach or misuse has a direct and serious threat to the privacy and confidentiality of his/her Personal Data.

### i. {#pt-i}

How to submit complaints to the Bureau.

## 2. {#para-2}

In all cases, the Controller shall, before starting the processing, provide the Data Subject with the information stipulated in paragraphs (b), (d) and (g) of Paragraph (1) of this Article.

## 3. {#para-3}

The Controller may reject the Data Subject's request to obtain the information mentioned in Paragraph (1) of this Article, if the following is established:

### a. {#pt-a}

The request is not related to the information referred to in Paragraph (1) of this Article, or it is excessively repetitive.

### b. {#pt-b}

The request conflicts with judicial procedures or investigations conducted by competent authorities.

### c. {#pt-c}

The request may negatively affect the efforts of the Controller to protect information security.

### d. {#pt-d}

The request affects the privacy and confidentiality of Personal Data of third parties.
