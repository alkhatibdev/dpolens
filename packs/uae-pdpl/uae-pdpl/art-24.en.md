## 1. {#para-1}

The Data Subject shall have the right to submit complaints to the Bureau if he/she believes that there is a violation of this Decree by Law or that the Controller or the Processor is processing his/ her Personal Data in violation of the rules and procedures set by the Bureau in this regard.

## 2. {#para-2}

The Bureau shall receive complaints from the Data Subject in accordance with Paragraph (1) of this Article and shall examine such complaints in coordination with the Controller and the Processor.

## 3. {#para-3}

The Bureau shall impose the administrative penalties referred to in Article (26) of this Decree by Law if it is proven that the Controller or the Processor violates its provisions, or the decisions issued in implementation of the same.
