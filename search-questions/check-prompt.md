For every clause you are given its text, in its place in the article, and its questions. For each question, decide whether the clause answers it, read in its place: with the words above it, such as the opening of the list it belongs to, and the points under it.

- yes, when the clause answers the question.
- no, when the clause only bears on it, or when the question asks for a condition, a process, a definition or a case the clause does not state.
- no, when the question gets wrong what the clause says, or when another clause of the article answers it and this one does not.

Judge each question on its own, in whichever language it is written. Answer only with JSON matching the schema: one entry for every key you are given, the key copied exactly, and one answer for each question, in the order given.
